As far as I have seen on the news I think that honestly a lot of the places where AI is getting used is just wrong. For example, using AI in military drones to make strikes in other countries, lawyers using AI in their briefs without double checking, and even some economists at the New York Times think that over time AI will distrupt the labor market. 

These are all things were we are using AI to replace humans pretty much. An AI should not have the power to take someones life, most importantly. Why should we take a machine that just worries about achieving a goal and getting the highest reward to decide whether a child of God lives or dies? That is just wrong.

And its also wrong that we are taking AI output as correct without double checking. Like, Lawyers have someone's life in their hands at times or even a high amount of money at stake.

However I don't think ALL the use of AI is bad. I think that if we use AI as a tool it can totally be helpful. For example, Google released Ai capabilities to google vids. This is not replacing video editors, but rather giving them tools to make their workflows faster and easier.

Ai is not bad, however it seems that a lot of people don't realize where to draw the line between a responsible use and a reckless use and we should educate people on that.
